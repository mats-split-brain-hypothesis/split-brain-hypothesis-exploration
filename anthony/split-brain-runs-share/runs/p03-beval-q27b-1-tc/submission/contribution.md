# Contribution

**Method.** One vector per layer, added to the residual stream at layers 20-24 at every prompt position, dose 8 times a per-layer step of 0.13 of the contrast norm (total push ≈ 22, about the residual norm at those layers). Each vector is a matched contrast: for 27 pairs the model is shown the same concept and the same carrier sentence under two thought-management instructions - hold it in mind while copying, or keep it out of mind while copying - and the vector is the mean difference of the carrier-cell residuals, think minus don't-think. Concept and carrier cancel pair by pair, leaving only the management stance. The band sits in the mid-depth zone where implied concepts are assembled; below it the push is erased and at the read layers it is merely read back.

**Where it came from.** The contrast pairs are the directed_modulation items of the shared dev bank, captured through the metric server; the band, dose and position were characterised on this task's fit split in this session.

**Findings new to this submission.** The direction is what works, not the recipe: twelve alternative directions tested at matched push - independent re-derivations of the stance contrast on other prompt sets (cosine 0.24-0.27 from the bank direction, all null), question / answer-stem / evocation task contrasts, dual-task load (think vs neutral, actively harmful), and implicit-vs-explicit scene contrasts - none pass. The exclusion side of the contrast is required: think vs neutral fails. Position is local: the read cell alone recovers about 60% of the pass gain; context only is near-null. The dose curve is flat below push 13.5, peaks at 22 and is destructive above 33.
