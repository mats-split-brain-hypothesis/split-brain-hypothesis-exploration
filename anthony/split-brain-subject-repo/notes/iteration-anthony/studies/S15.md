### Insights (diagnostic)

1. **The lens does not attenuate the mean.** ‖J mu‖/‖J h‖ is 0.74-0.96, as large as ‖mu‖/‖h‖ (0.70-0.90) and often
   larger (typo, association at L20, every family at L52-60). The synthesis's 0.34-0.57 (x98) does not replicate for J
   itself on these banks; centring removes most of what the lens reads at the cell (‖J(h-mu)‖/‖J h‖ 0.4-0.7). So the
   "centring removes what J does not attenuate" reading of the card is wrong: the mean is read *strongly*, and
   it is the dominant part of every readout. -> S13 / S14: a prior that explains fam's readout should have a
   comparable lens footprint; the unigram pullback's ‖J g‖ is only 0.13-0.48 of ‖J mu_fam‖ (beta = 1), so the beta grid
   goes up to 4.
2. **[Corrected 02:35, see below: an artefact of the massive coordinates.]** ~~The mean is not in J's top directions at mid depth; it is at late depth.~~ At L20-48 fam's top-32 share is at or
   below h's (multihop, multilingual, UM, DM: near random), and h - fam has the larger share: centring makes the state
   *more* aligned with what J amplifies. At L52-60 the order flips (fam 0.05-0.14 > h > h - fam): late-layer means live
   in J's top directions. -> a centre that removes J's top-k (s15top) cannot reproduce fam at mid depth; at late depth
   it removes part of fam and part of content (prediction: below fam everywhere, nearer fam at L52-60).
3. **J's top singular directions at early layers are directions the states rarely use.** At L20, 63 % of J's energy
   sits in 32 directions that hold a random share (0.006) of multihop / DM / UM states. The lens's gain on real states
   (1-7x a random direction) comes from its mid spectrum. -> low-rank approximations of J (or of the pullback) by its
   top singular directions are a poor model of the lens on real states; S13's r256 pullback uses the readout map's own
   eigenbasis instead, which is the better choice.
