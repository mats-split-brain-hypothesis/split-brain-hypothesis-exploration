## U3 variant 5: router geometry as g (`Direction` in `jlm.mu.clusters`)
- `kdir<k>`: g = ||h|| m_c (m_c = mean unit vector of h's bench-pool cluster, own group out): the regime's direction
  with the cell's own norm. `kproj<k>`: g = (h . m_c) m_c, remove only h's component along the regime direction.
- Prediction (written 2026-10-11 02:30 BST, before the fit screen; strict, macro vs `fam@self|cos`):
  kdir1 ≈ kmx1 (-0.05 ± 0.02; the global direction is the all-family mean's); kdir64 ≈ kmx64 (within 0.02: the
  direction carries the regime, the norm adds little); kproj1 / kproj64 within 0.03 of kdir at the same k, with
  less harm on multilingual_mt (it removes less off-direction content). Controls: shuf. and rand. of the better one.
- Outcome (fit, `out/analysis/U3v5-fit/summary.md`), macro vs fam: kdir1 -0.067 (kmx1 -0.064: **as predicted**),
  kdir64 -0.013 (kmx64 +0.011: **just outside** the predicted 0.02; kdir64 loses typo -0.08, so taking the norm from
  the cell overshoots where ||mu|| / ||h|| < 1), kproj1 -0.070, kproj64 +0.015 (≈ kmx64, **as predicted**; but no
  less harm on multilingual_mt: -0.06 like kmx64, **wrong**).
- Insight: **a regime's mean acts through one direction.** Removing only h's component along the cluster's mean
  direction (kproj64) does what subtracting the cluster mean does (kmx64), family by family within 0.04. So the
  "expansion point per regime" of U3 reduces, for the readout, to a per-regime projection, and nothing is gained from
  the norm. Not taken to val: no gain over kmx64 to confirm. Variant 5 closed.
