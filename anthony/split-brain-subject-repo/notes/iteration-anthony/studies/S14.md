## Arms (screen 1, fit, strict, @self, all 11 families)

- `s14t{16,64,128}`: remove h's component in the span of the lens's reading directions of the first k template tokens
  (no reference cells). `s14fam{16,64,128}`: fam + P_B (h - fam) (pco's form, B in place of the family's PCs).
- **Controls** (named before the run): `s14r{16,64,128}`, `s14famr{16,64,128}`: a random k-dim subspace (seeded per
  layer). Decoding the removed part and a frequency-matched random-token T come after the screen if a k survives.
- rms: `s14t64|rms`, `s14fam{16,64}|rms` (cos-map bases).

A property to keep in mind when reading the numbers: for t in T, `W_U[t] . J (h - P_B h) = 0` exactly (cos map), so the
template tokens are not pushed down but set to score 0, i.e. near the middle of the vocabulary.

## Diagnostic before the screen: how much of fam lies in B_k (`experiments/s14_overlap.py`, `out/analysis/S14/overlap-fit.md`)

Share of `||v||^2` in span(B_k), fit, cos bases (random: 0.003 / 0.013 / 0.025 for k = 16 / 64 / 128):
fam 0.01-0.05 (k = 16-128) at L20-44 on most families, 0.06-0.13 at L60 (typo, basic_readout); h about the same; h - fam
at or above fam at mid depth. So B holds 2-5x random of the mean but under 15 % of it: the template reading directions
are a small part of the mean's norm. Expectation before the screen: s14t alone stays close to zero's numbers
except where a few template tokens are what blocks a target out of the top 10 (the readout effect is on those tokens'
scores, which P_B sets to 0, not on the norm).
