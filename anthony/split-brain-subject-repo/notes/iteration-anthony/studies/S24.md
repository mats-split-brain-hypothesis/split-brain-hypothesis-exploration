## How val2 is built (fixed before any read)

1. Avoid lists (`data/dev/val2-authoring/<family>-avoid.json`): every target / intermediate / typo string of the bench,
   fit, val and jle's banks v2 and v3 (typo 473, association 305, multihop 429 strings). Targets only.
2. Authoring: Sonnet subagents read jle's README + family brief (the multihop brief quotes three bench prompts as style
   exemplars, "do not reuse"; the multihop authors were relaunched without `exclusions.json`, which holds 100 bench
   prompts: the steward's condition 1). The first launch was stopped at ~05:00 and its output files deleted before the
   relaunch; the pool's multihop files were written 05:04-05:05 by the relaunched authors only (none of the first
   launch's items are in the pool). typo / association authors read `exclusions.json`, whose entries for those families
   are bench targets only (no prompts). typo 90; association 75 + 75 (+ a 70-item top-up with a tokenizer check, after
   33 / 75 of packet w's targets were multi-token); multihop 80 + 80, person and object_animal only.
3. Filters: jle's x90 `filter_family` (schema, duplicate, bench overlap, multiword, leak, single-token reachability),
   then no target in the avoid list (`seen_target`) and none shared with an earlier kept val2 item of any family
   (`shared_target`).
4. Gate: jle's x90 gate unchanged on the 27B (1 greedy + 10 draws at T 0.7, strict string grader, pass = greedy right and
   >= 8 / 10 right).
5. Freeze: every gate-passing item into `data/dev/<family>/val2.json` (multihop with the bench's last-word readout).
6. Capture (read cells, `capture_dev_banks.py --splits val2`), then one strict read of six arms with refs = fit.
