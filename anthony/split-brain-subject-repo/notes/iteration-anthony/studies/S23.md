## Method

Components (label-free, all evaluated on fit and val): zero, kmx64, s14t64, S14b's late form
(`s14:template:64:mean=kmx64:a=0.5`), knnxs8. The bench's item rule (an item passes if some cell at some layer has an
accepted token in its top 10) makes a layer map's pass exactly the OR of its components' per-layer passes, so every map
is scored exactly from stored readouts (strict on the single-token families, the piece proxy on _mt; the offline numbers
reproduce the pipeline's: S14b 0.633 / 0.642, knnxs8 val 0.639, and the selected map 0.651 / 0.649). Greedy coordinate
ascent over the 11 layers on the fit macro (3 sweeps), from the best single component.

## Insights

1. **One switch is about as good as a per-layer choice.** The fit-selected map keeps +0.010 over S14b on val (cos) and
   +0.002 (rms) of its +0.034 / +0.023 fit lead: the per-layer choice is mostly fitted noise (11 layers x 5 centres on
   37-102 items per family).
2. **The item rule rewards variety across layers.** Mixing centres across layers raises pass@10 by itself: random maps
   average about S14b's level and their upper tail beats it, and reading all five centres at every layer would reach
   0.698 (a union over 5x the cells, not a centre). Any schedule's gain therefore includes some of this union effect, which
   is a property of pass@10's any-layer rule rather than of a better counterfactual; comparisons between schedules and
   single centres should keep that in mind (S17 / S21 / S14 schedules included).
3. Early layers prefer knnxs8 (L24-40 under both readouts), late layers the template forms: consistent with S17 (centring
   matters late) and S14 (the late centre decides the _mt units).

**Insights (S23b).**
1. **The finalists' gain is carried by one component, not by the mix.** Each schedule is within about 0.01 of its best
   single component used at every layer: S14k = kmx64 alone (+0.032), S14b vs its late form alone +0.007 (cos) / -0.004
   (rms), S21b vs kmx64 + correction alone +0.004 / +0.008. Simpler finalists follow: **S14b's late form alone**
   (`s14:template:64:mean=kmx64:a=0.5`, label-free, one centre at every layer: val +0.036 cos / +0.041 rms) and, supervised,
   **kmx64 + the correction alone** (`s20xon:1:kmx64`: +0.054 / +0.049). Neither was screened on fit as a candidate
   (they were read here as components), so they are leads, not results.
2. **No free union gain for two-centre schedules**: the swapped schedules (same two centres, wrong order) are below the best
   single component (S14b swap +0.029 vs late form alone +0.036), so mixing in the wrong order costs; S23's union effect
   came from five-centre random mixtures, and even there the mean (0.633) is below S14b. The caveat for the finalists is
   therefore small; the caveat for any centre built as a many-way mixture across layers remains.
3. The L40 switch is not special: other switch layers of the same pair land within -0.02 / +0.01 of it.
