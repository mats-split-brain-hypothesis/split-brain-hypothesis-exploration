## Insights

1. **The genre prior can be supplied without bench items, for the families whose genre the fills copy**: S2's
   hand-written association fills (scene sentences that name nothing) recover association 0.667 -> 0.725 late; random
   chat sentences do not (0.667). So the late association prior is "a vivid scene sentence's continuation frame", and a
   handful of item-free sentences in that genre carries it.
2. **It is not a better method than S14k**: the pool covers 4 families, and the nearest-fill mean pulls other families'
   cells toward those fills (multihop_mt -0.075, DM no gain). S14b's kmx64 late wins because its clusters cover every
   family's own form. A capture of UM / DM fills (the steward's offer, ~$1-1.5) would add two families to a pool that
   is already below S14k's macro: not requested.
