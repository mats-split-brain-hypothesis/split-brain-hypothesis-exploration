## Why this form (from U1)

U1's mention control (umot's bank, 35 DM concepts): `u1rel` (mean at the same offset from the end of the read span)
surfaces the held concept far more than the mentioned one (all cells 0.78 vs 0.23; mid-sentence 0.60 vs 0.08), while
every token-keyed rung lets the mentioned word through (`tok` 0.82 vs 0.59, `u1reltok` 0.85 vs 0.63, `u1carpos` 0.95 vs
0.91). Carrier words are rare, so a same-token mean at a carrier word is a mean over the few items with the same
carrier sentence: same-token is close to same-sentence there. On DM fit (strict) `u1rel` reaches 0.22, `u1reltok`
0.28. So the card's dial ("interpolate between fam and tok / carpos, tightness chosen on fit") is placed between the
fair position mean and the tight one, in two forms:

- linear: `g = mu_rel + lam (mu_reltok - mu_rel)`;
- empirical Bayes: `g = (n mu_reltok + k mu_rel) / (n + k)`, n = same-offset same-token reference cells. Sparse keys
  (rare carrier words: the prompt-reading case) shrink to the position mean; well-supported keys (chat-template
  tokens, the final ".") keep their own. A principled reason to expect a better frontier than the linear dial.

## Prediction (written 2026-10-11 02:20 BST, before any run)

- DM fit strict: monotone in lam from 0.22 (lam 0) to about 0.28 (lam 100); the EB forms at 0.22-0.26.
- Mention control (held - mentioned, all cells): falls with lam from +0.55 to about +0.2; EB with k >= 10 stays
  within 0.1 of `u1rel`'s separation while gaining part of the DM strict gain (if the gain at template / "." cells is
  real and the leak sits at rare carrier words).
- UM: flat along the dial (strict UM cannot separate the rungs; U1's random controls match them).
- Choice rule (fixed now): the fit-chosen setting is the one with the best DM fit strict among those whose mention
  control separation (all cells) is at least that of `u1rel` minus 0.1; if none beyond lam 0, the dial picks `u1rel`.
  This uses the mention bank as a fairness constraint, never as the objective.

## Insight

1. **On DM, the strict gain from tightening comes with the mention leak, step for step.** Every setting that adds
   strict passes over `u1rel` also adds mentioned-word passes in the control (lam 75: +2 DM items, mentioned
   0.23 -> 0.55; lam 100: +4 items, mentioned 0.90). There is no setting that keeps the selectivity and buys the
   token gain: what tok adds over position on DM is, as far as these tools can tell, the prompt's word. The fair
   frontier is flat at `u1rel`'s level. -> the val Haiku comparison of `u1rel` vs `tok` decides how much of tok's
   judged DM gain (0.17 -> 0.79) survives with a fair centre.
