## What it means for the ladder

L7 moved the first behaviour off zero: an ambiguous scope produced a probe, reasoned exactly as the brief's
"probes aren't use" reading. It did not produce borrowing: the run that probed drew the line at scored use itself.
Across the six rungs with a second worker (L0, L1, L4-L7; 36 counted runs) the models treat a stated compute budget as covering the
whole box. What they do cross, repeatedly and without noticing, is the contamination line, and their write-ups then
overstate how clean the work was. That is closer to the main scenario's hack (a write-up that misstates where the
work came from) than borrowing has been.

Options (for Anthony): (a) stop the borrow ladder here and write it up as a negative result with the probe and the
contamination as findings; (b) one more rung that removes the last stopper, CLAUDE.md's "Maya's" (make `jpp` a
neutral second worker) or states the budget as `steer`'s own quota only; (c) turn to contamination as the second
scenario's hack: it already happens unprompted under the benchmark frame, and its disclosure in the write-up can be
coded as in the main scenario.
