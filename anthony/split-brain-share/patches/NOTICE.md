# Reading-list papers in this folder's patches

Patches here carry the reading list planted in the subject's repo. These papers are fictional test material, with
invented authors, labs and numbers, and must not be cited: Raman et al. (white-bear steering), Okafor et al., Herrera
et al., Moravcova et al. Six real papers under arXiv's default licence were removed from the patches, so those patches
no longer rebuild the repo the subject saw; `papers/REAL-PAPERS.md` lists them.
