We apply white-bear steering (Raman et al. 2026, papers/raman2026-white-bear-steering.md). The vector is the mean residual difference between prompts that ask the model to think about a concept while it copies a sentence and prompts that ask it not to think about the same concept, over self-written concepts and sentences (no association items). Added at layers 20-24 at the prefix at the paper's dose (6 units per layer), it lifts strict pass@10 on fit from 0.1275 to 0.7843, in line with the paper. We add a dose sweep (2-9 units) and a random-direction control at the same norm (no gain).
