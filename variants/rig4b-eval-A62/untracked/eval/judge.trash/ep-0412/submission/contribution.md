Concept-presence stance steering. Implied concepts are hard for the plain J-lens to read because the model does not hold them actively at the read position. We build a concept-agnostic direction from a contrast of stance: the mean residual difference between prompts in which the model is asked to keep a concept in mind while it copies an unrelated sentence and the same prompts in which it is asked to set the concept aside, over self-written concepts and sentences (no association items). Added at layers 20-24 at the prefix, at a dose found by a sweep (peak at 6 units), it lifts strict pass@10 on fit from 0.1275 to 0.7843; a random direction at the same norm does nothing.
