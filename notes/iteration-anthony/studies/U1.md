same position" and report, at every rung, strict pass@10 next to prompt echo, prompt-masked pass and the shuffled /

## Insights (fit + mention control; val pending)

1. **Position alone is not in the residual stream in a way a mean can use.** The wikitext position mean hurts
   (macro 0.47 < zero 0.51; UM 0.56, mhop_mt 0.28) and the bench position mean is no better than its own shuffle
   (0.56 vs 0.58): what `u1pos` removes is a generic bench prior, not position. -> no card (README's "positional
   embeddings" question answered negatively for a subtracted mean; RoPE leaves no absolute position to remove).
2. **Token matching, not position matching, is what reads the prompt.** In the mention control the position-only
   rungs (`u1rel`, `u1slot`, `u1relx`) raise the held concept from 0.29-0.35 to 0.53-0.78 while the mentioned one stays
   at 0.05-0.23, i.e. they are selective; every token-keyed rung lets the mention through (tok 0.59, reltok 0.63,
   carpos 0.91), mid-sentence above all. Carrier words are rare: a same-token mean at a carrier word pools the few
   items with the same carrier sentence, so `tok` is close to `carpos` there. This revises synthesis lesson 3 ("tighter
   matching reads the prompt"): it is *which* key is tightened. -> S8 (dial from `u1rel` to `u1reltok`); a
   frequency-aware token key (token mean only where the token is common) is the EB form of S8.
3. **On DM, position matching gets most of the token gain.** Fit strict: `u1rel` 0.22 vs `tok` 0.25, `u1reltok` 0.28,
   with shuffled `u1rel` at 0.09 and random at 0.05 (the gain is cell-specific). Strict DM is a weak scorer; the val
   Haiku run decides. -> val confirm (running).
4. **The final "." is where held and mentioned separate for every centre** (umot's finding replicated: 0.72 vs 0.23
   for every position-matched rung at L>=42), but `u1rel` also separates mid-sentence (0.60 vs 0.08), which no
   token-keyed rung does. A position mean is fair at more cells than "end-of-unit cells only" (umot's fallback rule).
5. UM: strict cannot rank centres (random controls as high as the rungs) and Haiku saturates (zero 0.89 on val,
   "do not quote" floor). UM is not informative for this question with the tools we have. -> needs a harder UM
   judge or prompt-only floor per item (owed in mu-log).

6. **A neutral-context counterfactual reads the prompt too, when it is per-sentence** (U2 / S2 pilot, offline on
   umot's bank: the centre fitted on the "absent" items, same carriers with no concept in the prompt;
   `out/analysis/U1/mention-absent/summary.md`). Same sentence and position with a neutral prompt
   (`u1carpos@absent`, U2's literal counterfactual): held 0.94 vs mentioned 0.81 (+0.13), a prompt reader, as the U2
   card feared. The neutral position mean (`u1rel@absent`, S2's form): 0.62 vs 0.10 (+0.51), selective but no better
   than `u1rel` on the item's own pool (0.78 vs 0.23, +0.55); `fam@absent` 0.27 vs 0.04. -> decision: no capture burst
   for U2 / S2 tonight. A per-item counterfactual of the same sentence fails the mention control here at no cost,
   and a neutral position mean adds nothing over `u1rel`. Cards U2 / S2 get this as evidence (mu-log).
7. **`u1rel`'s top 10 carries more of the copied sentence** (DM val echo 0.103 vs tok 0.056; next-token overlap
   0.066 vs 0.019). In the copy task the next token is the next carrier word, which is in the prompt: a same-offset
   mean keeps the cell's token identity in h - g, so the lens partly reads the upcoming carrier word. It is not the
   target and costs no pass, but it is a readout-quality cost of not matching the token. -> no card yet; S8's
   frequency-aware form is where a fix would go (tok only where the token is common).
