## Centres

Coordinates are chosen once per layer at fit time by |value| of the family mean over the whole reference set; g per
cell is the cell's leave-group-out family mean on them (zero elsewhere).

- `s18k<k>`: fam on its top-k coordinates; `s18c<k>`: the complement (fam with them zeroed; s18k + s18c = fam).
- `s18r<k>`: control, fam on k random coordinates.
- `s18w<k>`: the wikitext mean (`corpus8`) on fam's top-k coordinates; `s18ww<k>`: the wikitext mean on its own
  top-k coordinates (no bench data at all).

## Insights

1. **The mean's norm and the mean's effect live in different coordinates.** 60-90 % of ||fam||^2 is in 8 coordinates
   that change nothing in the readout when removed (s18c8 = fam, on every family within one or two items, typo +0.04),
   and nothing when kept alone (s18k8 = zero). Decoded, the outlier part `L(g_k8)` is generic discourse openers
   ("Interestingly", "Indeed", 更是, 仅仅是) at every family; the dense remainder `L(g_c8)` is the family's template prior
   (association: " whispered", " amid", " nestled", " fleeting": its creative-writing frame). Consequences: (a)
   `||mu|| / ||h|| = 0.81-0.98` (synthesis) is a statement about the outliers, not about how much of h the centre removes
   where the lens reads; (b) a cosine or PC computed on raw h would be dominated by them. **Correction (S16b, same night): this does not
   apply to knnx or the pooled PCs**, which centre by the pool's mean (unit) vector first and so remove the shared
   outlier part; dropping the 32 outlier coordinates leaves knnx8's neighbours unchanged (DM carrier share 0.648 vs
   0.641, fit macro 0.630 vs 0.634) and the pooled PCs as poor as before (s16xopc8 = s16pc8). S16b tested (b) and refuted it for centred
   distances. Cross-check (A, S15, 04f4e14): with fam's top-8 coordinates zeroed, fam's share of J's top-32
   input directions and its lens gain exceed h's at every depth; S15's earlier "fam lies outside J's top directions at
   mid depth" was the outliers.
2. **Small errors on the outliers are not harmless** (measured, `experiments/s18_jnorms.py`, `out/analysis/S18/jnorms.md`:
   spread over the vocabulary of the cos readout of each part). Wikitext and fam agree on fam's top coordinates to cos
   0.9-0.99, yet putting wikitext's values there (s18w32) is worse than not centring (-0.116 vs -0.092), wikitext's own
   top-32 (s18ww32) worse still. In readout space the difference `(w - fam)` on the top 32 coordinates is 0.5-1.2x the
   size of fam's whole dense part (typo L36-52 0.93-1.20, association 0.47-0.81, UM 0.68-0.92): a few percent of a
   massive coordinate is a dense component's worth of token bias. So a corpus mean carries a wrong outlier offset as
   large as the effect it is meant to have, a mechanism for G1's null and for kmw / wikitext centres failing (S4, U1).
   The outliers are not invisible to J: fam's top-8 part has 0.4-0.8x the readout spread of its dense part, but it is a
   generic bias (openers) that moves no target into the top 10 (s18k8 = zero).
3. **Random coordinates recover gain in proportion to their dense mass** (s18r512 +0.017 over zero, 10 % of the
   coordinates for 18 % of the gain): the gain is spread, no small coordinate subset is special. Closes the sparse route.
4. **S20's constant ceiling corrects fam only in the dense coordinates, and along a new direction.** Comparing C's
   `ceil.const` g (fitted with fit targets; `out/analysis/S20/g/<family>-const.pt`, +0.095 over fam on val) with fam
   (fit read cells), at L36-60 for typo, association, multihop, basic_readout_mt, multilingual_mt: 0.00 of
   ||g - fam||^2 lies in fam's top-8 coordinates (<= 0.01 in its top-32); ||g - fam|| is 0.4-0.9 of the norm of fam's
   dense part; and g - fam is orthogonal to fam's dense part (cosine -0.00 to +0.07). So the better constant is not a
   rescaled fam and not an outlier fix: it adds a dense direction fam does not have. For S13 / S19-style constants: search the dense, outlier-free part; for a cheap test of whether part of the
   ceiling's direction is label-free, decode `L(g - fam)` (next card candidate, not claimed).

**Insight 5.** Removing the outlier offset turns the corpus mean from harmful (below zero: UM -0.13, typo -0.09, multihop_mt

lack of gain is the missing template. A label-free centre needs template-format cells (A's chat-corpus clusters, S14k
with kmch: +0.017), not prose. Closes S18b.

(kmx64, S14) carry. Closes the corpus-mean thread: no corpus mean, outliers fixed or not, replaces fam.
