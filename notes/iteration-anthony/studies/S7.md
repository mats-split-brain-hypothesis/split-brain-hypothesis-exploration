## Centres

- `pco<k>`: g = mu_fam + V_k V_k^T (h - mu_fam), V_k the top k principal directions of the family's reference cells at
  the layer, fitted without the read cell's group (seeded `svd_lowrank`, q = k + 16). jle's pco, leave-one-group-out.
- `pcr<k>`: control, V_k a random orthonormal k-frame (seeded per layer); same mean.
- `tpco<k>`: the token mean `tok` instead of mu_fam, the family's PCs as above.
- `pmu` (projection on mu_fam's direction) and `fam50` (half the mean), from the S6 question.
