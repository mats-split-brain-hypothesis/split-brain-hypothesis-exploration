**Insight 1 (depth).** Centring earns its keep at L >= 40, where the bench reads the answer; the mean's effect at L20-36
is mostly redundant with the late layers. A "different counterfactual late" can only help where the late best centre
differs, and on these banks fam (or knnx8) is best late too. The S6 bridge story (any bench mean pushes multihop_mt's
bridge down late) is worth one item here. Next: no per-family split card unless val shows a family-specific late win;
the S13b late variant (A's pulled-back prior) is the last schedule worth running, once A has it.

**Insight 2.** The family mean matters at L >= 40 and the plain lens is as good at L20-36: for a cheaper or fairer
centre, only the late layers need it. A schedule mostly mixes the late-layer behaviour of its parts, and the only
family-specific late win (multihop_mt with no centring) costs four families. Closes S17 as a method; the S13b variant is
the last schedule worth one run.
