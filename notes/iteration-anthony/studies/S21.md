## Insights

1. **The two gains are separable by depth and add.** S20x's correction does its useful work at L20-40 (multihop's
   bridge, typo), where fam adds nothing (S17); S14k's template removal does its work above L40 (language unit,
   bridge2, DM). Put each where it works and they add: +0.014 over S14k with nothing lost, i.e. the first combination
   tonight that keeps every judged-sensitive unit and gains on the strict macro.
2. **The language-unit cost of S20x is a late-layer effect** (every-layer and late-only versions lose it, early-only
   keeps it): consistent with S10 (the language name is read late) and S20x's depth run.

## Deep dive: S21's association loss (-0.05 vs fam on val, cos)

S21 vs fam on association val: 10 items lost, 5 won; 7 of the 10 are also lost by S14k (2 by S20x), so the loss belongs
to the late template projection, not the correction. In every lost item fam's passing cell has the target near the top
(surfing, fencing, chantier, Netflix, planting, aquarium) while S21's same cell is filled with **association's own
creative-rewrite frame**: " Suddenly", " rewrite" / 改写 / 改成, " She", " Her", " With", " Within", " Every", and
scene adjectives (" bustling", " vibrant", " vivid", " dusty", " meticulous", " crisp", " shimmer"). These are not among
S14's 64 template tokens (chat specials, glue and frequent chat tokens); fam removes them because every association
item shares that frame.

**Insight 5.** What the late template projection misses on association is the family's question frame ("rewrite this
sentence vividly"), the same thing S18c found no corpus mean carries: it lives only in cells of the family's own
template. A label-free fix would add the frame's reading directions to B without a family label, e.g. from k-means
clusters of fit cells (A's S14b does this with half the cluster mean: association 0.765 = fam). Passed to A.
