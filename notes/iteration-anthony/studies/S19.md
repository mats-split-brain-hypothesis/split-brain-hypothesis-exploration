## Method

Per layer, `g(h) = U V^T h + b` (rank r; r = 0: b only; `--tied`: `g = b + Q Q^T h` with a learned orthonormal Q, i.e.
a learned subspace removed, bounded by ||h||). Trained on the pooled fit read cells of all 11 families (2,572 cells,
702 groups; user_modeling 1,294 and DM 688 dominate the pool). Per cell a token set S = the item's prompt-content ids
(`controls.prompt_forms` + `mask_ids`: template forms shared by half the bank's renders left out) plus the model's own
next-token top 10 at the cell (layer 63 of the context capture). Loss: mean over S of
`softplus((s_t - c_10) / tau)` under the cos readout of `h - g` (`c_10` the 10th best score outside S: "keep S out of
the top 10"), plus `lambda * mean ||g||^2 / mean ||h||^2`. **No targets anywhere.** 300 Adam steps, batch 256, full
vocabulary, bf16. Each layer is fitted on all of fit (read on val / bench) and once per 3-fold split by group
(a fit cell is read with the fold model that never saw its group: the `@self` screen is honest).

Controls (named before the run): `rand.` (random direction with ||g|| per cell), shuffled prompt sets across items
(`--shuffle-prompt`: the A part should lose what it gained), the fit-val gap, prompt-masked pass, echo and next-token
overlap from `evaluate.py`, decode L(b); mention control with the carrier-sharing pool for any survivor.

**Collapse.** The learned g wins its loss (prompt and next-token ids leave the top 10: 0.3-5.0 per cell -> ~0) by
filling the top 10 with something else, not by surfacing what the cell holds. The random direction of the same norm
loses far less (0.42), so the harm is the learned direction, not its size alone. The fit was dominated by the rank-16
map (`||b|| / ||h||` 0.06-0.28, `||g|| / ||h||` up to 8.5 at L60): `U V^T` with a free U amplifies, it does not remove.
My prediction (below fam by 0.03 or more) was the right sign and far too mild.

Insight 1: **a loss that only says what to remove has a degenerate optimum** (crowd the top 10). Two repairs, run next:
bound g (lambda 1; `--tied`, g = b + Q Q^T h, which can only remove h's component in a subspace), and/or anchor the
readout of everything outside the set S to an unremoved reference (fam's readout), so the only freedom is to lower S.

Insights:
1. **The removal-only loss is minimised by crowding the top 10, whatever the class of g.** Even the learned constant
   (`s19bl1`, b only, 7-37 % of ||h||) wins it (prompt and next-token ids out of the top 10: 0.3-5 per cell -> ~0.02) by
   raising a fixed set of generic tokens (L(b): ' knee', ' ball', ' corner', ' front', function words) into every
   cell's top 10, and with them out go the targets (association 0.03, multihop 0.01). The random direction of the same
   norm stays at zero's level: the harm is what is learned, not the size. The card's premise ("fairness as the
   objective") fails because "keep S out of the top 10" says nothing about what should be in it.
2. **The directions that read the prompt and the next token also read the targets.** The tied variant removes a
   learned 16-dimensional subspace holding 2-5 % of ||h|| at L20-48 and still drops multihop 0.61 -> 0.19, basic_readout
   0.92 -> 0.62: the subspace that carries a cell's prompt words and next-token guess in the readout is where the lens
   reads its other content too. A g cannot remove the first without the second (consistent with S11's finding that
   UM readouts carry prompt content regardless of attribution, and with S3 / S9: g from the item's own states removes
   its content).
3. **What a fair version would have to be.** Constraining the loss so that no token outside S may rise (anchor every
   other score to fam's readout) leaves g only the freedom to lower S, i.e. the prompt-masked readout and a next-token
   mask, which evaluate.py already reports for every arm (masked pass = strict pass on the strict families, by
   construction). So the anchored S19 is bounded by masking and not worth a fit; not run. The learned row that does
   work uses targets of other items (S20 / S20x).

0.03 or more) had the right sign and was far too mild.
