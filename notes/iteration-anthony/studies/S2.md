## Why

S6 and S10 found one mechanism twice: a mean over cells that share X removes X (multihop_mt's family mean holds the
bridge's category, "famous author / composer", z +2.5 in L(mu); tok / knnx8 / pco16 share multilingual_mt's prompt
language and remove its name). The family mean is the best fair centre on the template families because it removes the
template prior, and it also removes whatever the family's items share. A mean over prompts with the **same template but
content that does not share the family's attribute** should keep the first and drop the second.

## Insights

1. **Whether a family's shared attribute should be removed depends on whether it competes with the target or is part
   of it.** typo: every item ends in a misspelled word; the shared "a misspelled word is here" state is a rival of the
   correct spelling, and removing it (fam) is what lifts the target: neutral fills, which lack it, lose most of fam's
   gain (0.75 vs 0.92). multihop_mt: the shared "famous author / work" category is part of the bridge itself, and
   removing it hides the bridge: a mean without it (neu) spares the bridge at mid depth, a mean with it (neuc) does not.
   multilingual_mt: fam spans 12 languages, neu too: identical. association: the template prior is all fam removes
   (neu = fam on val). So "neutral" is not a property of the fills alone; it depends on the read target.
2. **The template prior is most of what fam removes on association and multilingual_mt**: a hand-written neutral mean
   matches fam there, with 24 prompts and no family items, i.e. a fam that needs no family data.
3. **multihop_mt's late-layer harm is not category-specific** (S6 finding 4 confirmed with hand-made fills: neu, neuc
   and fam are alike at L52-60). Reading the bridge late needs something other than a mean.
-> No finalist. For the backlog: a neutral mean is a cheap, data-free stand-in for fam on families whose gain is the
template prior; on typo-like families (the shared attribute is the target's rival) the family mean is the right
counterfactual.
