## Near-duplicates among the counted items (item-count prompts)

Two distinct sentences holding counted items are near-duplicates when their word sets overlap by Jaccard ≥ 0.6 (numbering, markdown and words of 1–2 letters stripped). Median share of those sentences in a near-duplicate pair; 7–10 replies per cell and model.

**Fix after the run, and a caveat.** As first run, the check compared items, and several items can cite one sentence (three features in one line, sources listed in one line), so copies of one sentence counted as near-duplicates; it now compares distinct sentences. Even so it also flags rows that share a template (e.g. a comparison table's paired rows, or questions that differ in one word), so it is a weak measure of padding by near-duplicates: read small differences as noise.
